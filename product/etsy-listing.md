# Etsy listing: the printable kit

Everything to paste into a new listing on **etsy.com/shop/GulfCoastHomeCare**.

Nothing here is generated, so edit it freely. What it points at *is* generated:
run `python build_printables.py` and the PDF appears in `product/`.

**This is the paid product at $12.99.** The free product is the three calendar
feeds on gulfcoasthomemaintenance.com, which are not sold anywhere. Etsy field
limits are noted where they bite.

---

## Listing type

**Digital, instant download.** Answer *yes* to "Is this a digital file?" during
setup. There is no shipping profile and no physical variant.

## Files to upload

Two, in this order. Etsy allows five, and both are well under the 20 MB cap.

| File | |
|---|---|
| `product/gulf-coast-home-maintenance-kit.pdf` | ~390 KB, the print version |
| `product/gulf-coast-home-maintenance-kit-fillable.pdf` | ~1.1 MB, type and save |

Separate files, not a zip. A zip makes someone on a phone do work before they
can see what they bought, and it hides the fact that there are two versions.

The print version goes first, because it is what most buyers want and Etsy lists
files in upload order.

## Photos

Etsy crops the search-grid thumbnail to **4:3**, which slices the title off a
portrait page. Compose the first image at 4:3 rather than screenshotting a page.

Put **UNDATED, NEVER EXPIRES** on that first image. Shoppers scan images before
they read titles, so the image is where that objection actually gets killed.
The title is the backup.

Suggested order: the 4:3 hero, the cover, the Watch List (the page people keep),
a month page, the May hurricane page, the Gulf Coast Year diagram, one of the
conditional sections, and the free phone calendars last as a bonus.

## Price

**$12.99.**

Etsy takes roughly $0.20 to list, 6.5% of the sale, and about 3% plus $0.25 to
process payment, so $12.99 nets about $11.31.

Why not lower: search volume is the binding constraint, not price. The obvious
term, `home maintenance checklist printable`, gets 47 searches a month. You
cannot make up the difference on units, so units are the wrong lever. At $7.99
a $6,000 year needs about 885 sales. At $12.99 it needs 531.

Worth considering: list at $12.99 and run a launch sale. Etsy shows a
strikethrough and a discount badge, which helps conversion, and ending the sale
leaves you at your real price without having to justify a rise.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will this work outside the Gulf Coast?**
Some of it. The structure works anywhere, but the timing, the lifespans and
about half the tasks are specific to long humid summers, termite pressure and
hurricane season. Inland Gulf South, most of it still applies. Further north,
the Watch List is where the value is.

**Is it dated?**
No, deliberately. There is no year and no weekday grid, so the same printout
works every year. Each task has a box to tick.

**Can I type into it, or do I have to print it?**
Either. There are two files. The print version is for printing and writing on by
hand. The fillable version lets you type into the Watch List and tick boxes on
screen, then save. Saving works in Adobe Reader and most desktop PDF apps; some
phone apps will let you type but cannot save, so use a computer if you want to
keep it.

**Do I need a color printer?**
No. Plain US Letter paper, and black and white loses only the level colors,
which are labeled anyway.

**What are the free phone calendars?**
Three subscribe-able calendars, one per level, that put the same twelve months
into Google, Apple or Outlook and repeat every year. The last page of the PDF
shows you where. They are free and need no purchase.

**Can I give this to my clients?**
Yes, and please do. Print a stack for closings. The only ask is that you send
people the link rather than the file, so they always get the current version.
