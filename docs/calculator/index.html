All four start at replaced on schedule, which assumes whoever owned the house before you kept up with it. That is the most generous of the three guesses, on purpose. What it means is that the answer above is the best case, and the real one is rarely better than the best case.

  • Roof, architectural shingle 15 yrs typical
  • A/C condenser, the outdoor unit 11 yrs typical
  • Water heater, tank 9 yrs typical
  • Air handler or furnace 16 yrs typical

Lifespans are Gulf South figures. Heat, humidity and hard UV age a house faster than the national averages assume, and these are the same numbers printed in the kit. They are guidelines rather than guarantees: a well-maintained roof outlives a neglected one, and a bad one fails early.
