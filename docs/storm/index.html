Storm prep

Storm season

Hurricanes are part of life on the Gulf Coast. Preparation is what turns chaos into a plan, and the plan has four parts, only one of which happens while the wind is blowing.

01 / The shape of it

Four parts, one plan

Most storm checklists cover the first part and stop. The money, it turns out, lives in the last two.

Before the storm

Insurance verified while the map is empty, an inventory of what you own, supplies, and the checklist you built while calm.

During the storm

Safety, communication, and the evacuation decision. The one part nobody can paper over, and the part the rest exists to shrink.

After the storm

Damage assessment with the camera before the cleanup, and documentation of everything, because wind and flood are different policies.

Insurance and claims

Policies and deductibles in dollars, deadlines, and the claim itself: the phase that decides what actually gets paid.
